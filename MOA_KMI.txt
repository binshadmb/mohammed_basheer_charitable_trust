MEMORANDUM OF ASSOCIATION
OF
KHAGATARA HEALTH INSTITUTE
(A Section 8 Non-Profit Company under the Companies Act, 2013)

3(a) THE MAIN OBJECTS TO BE PURSUED BY THE COMPANY UPON ITS INCORPORATION ARE:

1. To establish, operate, maintain, and manage non-profit healthcare facilities, dialysis centers, diagnostic laboratories, multi-specialty hospitals, and research institutes to provide affordable, subsidized, or free medical relief, renal care, life-support services, and health management to the underprivileged, economically backward, and general public.

2. To organize, conduct, and promote community health awareness programs, free medical camps, organ donation drives, diagnostic screenings, and therapeutic care initiatives aimed at preventing and mitigating chronic illnesses and kidney-related diseases.

3. To accept, receive, mobilize, and utilize grants, donations, subscriptions, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funds, and government assistance solely for the advancement, expansion, maintenance, and execution of the charitable and healthcare objectives of the Institute without any motive for profit distribution or dividend payout to its members/directors.
